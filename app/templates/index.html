About EcoTrack

Solid waste management affects environmental cleanliness, community well-being, and quality of life. EcoTrack provides a centralized, digital channel to report unattended waste, overflowing bins, and improper disposal — then track progress until resolved.

This is an academic prototype developed as a BCA final-year project. It is not connected to live municipal authorities or waste collection services.
